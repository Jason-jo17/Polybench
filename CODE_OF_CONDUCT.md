# Code of conduct

PolyBench is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want everyone who takes part, whether by filing an issue, reviewing code or asking a first question, to be treated with respect.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Explain the reasoning behind review feedback.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Accept that decisions sometimes go against you, and help carry them out.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or pushing on after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Jason-jo17). Reports are kept confidential.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block someone from the project. Responses will be proportionate, and people will be told why action was taken.
